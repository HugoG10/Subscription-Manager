About Wallet Worth

Wallet Worth is an independent subscription tracker. It is not a bank, and it is not a company with a staff or investors behind this page.

Recurring charges are easy to forget because they are small and automatic. Wallet Worth is a place to write them down, see the monthly equivalent, and notice the date before the next charge.

You can type plans in yourself. If you want, you can upload a CSV statement or connect a US bank account through Plaid so charges can be reviewed and recurring ones suggested. The app does not move money and does not cancel plans.

The project is still early. Email verification and password reset by email are not built yet.

FAQ

What is Wallet Worth?

A private account where you track subscriptions, their cost, and their next renewal date.

Is Wallet Worth free?

There is no price in the app today. That is a description of the current build, not a promise that it will never charge.

How does Wallet Worth work?

You create an account, add plans, and the dashboard totals the active ones and lists renewals that fall inside your reminder window.

Can I manage multiple subscriptions?

Yes. The list holds as many plans as you add.

Can I manually add subscriptions?

Yes. Use Add subscription. You can also load a sample set.

Does Wallet Worth automatically cancel subscriptions?

No.

Can I receive renewal reminders?

The app shows reminders when you are signed in. It does not send email or text reminders.

Is my personal information secure?

Passwords are hashed. Bank access tokens are encrypted before they are stored. This page does not claim a certification or a specific security standard.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No.

How does Plaid work with Wallet Worth?

If you choose Connect your bank, Plaid handles the bank sign-in. Wallet Worth receives an access token and can read posted transactions to suggest recurring charges. It does not receive your bank password.

Can I edit or remove subscriptions?

Yes. Each plan has Edit and Remove. Remove all clears the list for that account.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from Account after you sign in. That deletes the login, plans, reviews, and stored charges for that account.

How do I report a bug?

Use the contact form and choose Bug. The message is saved for the owner. It is not emailed automatically.

How can I contact support?

Use the contact form. There is no separate support inbox published here.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: not yet approved.

Information collected at registration

Email address, a hashed password, the time the account was created, and an optional note of the link that led you to sign up (source, medium, and campaign). The app does not verify that the email inbox is yours.

Account information stored

The account id, email, password hash, last time the session was opened, whether an administrator marked the account as a test account, and a review if you submit one.

Subscription information

Name, category, price, billing cycle, renewal date, status, reminder window, usage rating, last-used date, and notes you type.

Financial information

If you upload a CSV, the app stores the posted date, amount, and description of charges it could parse. If you connect a bank, it stores an encrypted Plaid access token, the institution name, and posted transaction dates, amounts, and descriptions used to find recurring charges. It does not store your bank password.

How information is used

To show your own dashboard, detect possible subscriptions, and, for the owner, to count registrations. Registration counts treat example.com addresses as test accounts.

Authentication

Passwords are hashed with scrypt. The session cookie is HttpOnly. There is no password-reset email.

Plaid

Plaid is optional. In production the app requests transaction history. Plaid, not Wallet Worth, collects the bank login. Disconnect removes the item at Plaid when that call succeeds, then deletes the imported bank charges stored here. Statement uploads are kept until you delete the account or remove those plans.

Analytics

The owner can see registration totals. The app does not run a visitor analytics product and does not use cross-site fingerprinting. A first-touch source is saved in this browser and sent only if you create an account.

Cookies

The session cookie keeps you signed in. Attribution is stored in localStorage under ww-attribution, not in a third-party cookie.

Retention and deletion

Data stays until you delete the account or the owner removes the database. Delete account removes the user, subscriptions, charges, bank items, dismissals, and reviews stored for that id. A Plaid item is revoked when the disconnect call succeeds.

Privacy rights and contact

A formal privacy-rights process, region, and contact address are not set. Use the contact form and choose Account. The owner still needs to name the legal entity and the request process before treating this page as final.
